ozen

Every meeting, remembered. On your Mac.

ozen (אוזן, “ear”) listens to your calls and the room, writes down who said what in Hebrew and English, and lets an AI assistant answer questions about the meeting while it happens. No bot joins your call. No audio leaves your Mac.

100% free. No account, no subscription, no trial.
Download for Mac Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 15+ · then follow Install

What ozen does for you

Writes everything down

Every word of the call and of the people in the room, in Hebrew or English, a few seconds after it's said.

Knows who's talking

Recognizes voices. Tell it once who someone is and it remembers them in every future meeting.

Gets smarter as you go

Fix a misheard word and ozen learns it. Fix the same mistake twice and it corrects it by itself.

Ask AI mid-meeting

One click opens an AI assistant that has read the meeting so far and can see your screen.

Records only when you want

Always, only during calls, or by place: on at the office, off at home.

Private by design

Transcription happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and no one gets an “ozen joined the meeting” notice.

Install

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return) and paste each command. About 10 minutes, once.

  1. Apple's developer tools. Click Install in the window that pops up. Already installed? Move on.
    xcode-select --install
  2. Homebrew, the standard way to add tools to a Mac. Skip if you have it.
    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"
  3. The tools ozen runs with.
    brew install uv ffmpeg
  4. Download ozen and add it to your Applications. It unpacks into a folder named ozen in your home folder.
    curl -fL https://pub-c924dc0ced674b94a6c670ac43ee914b.r2.dev/ozen-latest-macos-arm64.tar.gz | tar -xz -C ~ && ~/ozen/target/release/ozen app

ozen appears in your menu bar. The first time it transcribes it downloads its speech models (about 7 GB). To update later, run step 4 again. Next: your first meeting.

Free, for real

ozen costs nothing and asks for nothing: no sign-up, no email, no usage limits, no paid tier. It runs entirely on your own Mac, so there are no servers for us to charge you for.
